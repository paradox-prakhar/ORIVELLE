About the journey

Ten days, moving north to south at walking speed. You'll live in Seoul, Jeonju & Gyeongju, and Busan — a capital that never exhales, two old towns that only exhale, and a port city that smells of salt and grilled eel.

Routes, tickets, timings, the right queue at the right hour — all of it is already solved. We tell you where to stand, what to order, and when to say nothing at all, so you can just be somewhere.

days 1 — 3

Seoul

Palace courtyards at opening hour, the print shops of Euljiro, a late bowl of noodles under signage older than we are. One dawn hike up Inwangsan.

days 4 — 6

Jeonju & Gyeongju

A night on a heated floor in a hanok, bibimbap where it was invented, then the Silla tombs and stone pagodas of Gyeongju under falling blossom.

days 7 — 10

Busan

Jagalchi market at 6am, the painted terraces of Gamcheon, a temple built on the rocks above the sea, and two unplanned days that are yours.
